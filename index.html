Time to Inbox (TTI): How Fast Email Reaches the Inbox

Time to inbox is the time between sending an email and its arrival in the recipient's inbox. These guides explain what it measures, how to measure it yourself, and what slows delivery down.

What is time to inboxSee Forward Email's live TTI

What is time to inbox?

Time to inbox (TTI) is the number of seconds between the moment an email is sent and the moment it lands in the recipient's inbox. A healthy TTI is a few seconds. Minutes or hours usually point to a queue, a retry, or a filter on the way.

Postmark, which publishes TTI for its own sending, defines it as "the length of time it takes from when an email is sent from a client until it is logged as delivered" (Postmark TTI page). Forward Email measures the same thing by sending test messages to real mailboxes and timing how long each one takes to show up over IMAP.

TTI matters most for email that someone is waiting for:

  • password reset links and sign-in links
  • one-time codes for two-factor authentication
  • account verification and order confirmation emails
  • monitoring alerts and incident notifications
  • replies in a live conversation or support thread

Common causes of slow delivery, with numbers

Most delays come from a temporary rejection followed by a retry. The retry schedule of the sending server then decides how long the message waits. These figures come from standards and published configuration defaults.

Delay sources and their documented timings
CauseDocumented timingSource
Retry after a temporary failure (any SMTP sender)Retry interval "SHOULD be at least 30 minutes"; give up after at least 4 to 5 daysRFC 5321, section 4.5.4.1
Postfix deferred queueRetries between 300 seconds and 4000 seconds apart; queue scanned every 300 seconds; message returned after 5 daysPostfix postconf(5)
Greylisting at Forward EmailFirst message from a new sender is deferred with a 450 code until 5 minutes have passedForward Email FAQ
Forward Email TTI alert thresholdEngineers are emailed when a test delivery takes 10 seconds or longer, or does not arrivejobs/tti.js

A message that is greylisted by the receiver and then retried on a 30 minute schedule arrives about 30 minutes late, even though nothing is wrong with it. See what affects email delivery speed for each cause and what you can change.

Forward Email publishes its time to inbox

Forward Email runs a monitor that sends test emails to mailboxes at major email providers and records how long each one takes to arrive. The results are public on the Forward Email time to inbox page, with a 24-hour chart, and the code that produces them is public on GitHub.

Each run measures two paths per provider: a direct message, and a message sent through a forwarding address that ends up in the same mailbox. The numbers change with every test run, so this site links to the live page instead of copying values that would go stale.
