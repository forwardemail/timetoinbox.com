# Privacy Policy

> TimeToInbox collects no personal data. The site has no cookies, analytics, trackers, forms or JavaScript, and every file loads from timetoinbox.com.

Source: https://timetoinbox.com/privacy/

## Privacy summary

TimeToInbox does not collect, store or share personal data. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and loads no fonts, images or scripts from other domains.

## What the site does not do

- No cookies, local storage or other browser storage.
- No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or session recording.
- No JavaScript. Pages are plain HTML and one stylesheet.
- No forms, accounts or newsletter sign-ups.
- No third-party requests. Fonts are your system fonts, and every image and stylesheet is served from timetoinbox.com. A Content Security Policy in each page blocks scripts and outside resources.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare

Requests to timetoinbox.com pass through Cloudflare, which runs as a CDN and proxy in front of GitHub Pages and processes the IP address of each visitor ([Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)). Your browser still only talks to timetoinbox.com.

GitHub Pages hosts the files. GitHub states that "when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes" ([GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages)). GitHub keeps those logs under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your data and your choices

Because the site stores nothing about you, there is no account to delete and no data to export. You do not need to accept or refuse cookies, and blocking JavaScript changes nothing on these pages. To ask about server or proxy logs, contact GitHub or Cloudflare through their privacy policies.

If you email the Forward Email team about this site, your message is handled under the [Forward Email privacy policy](https://forwardemail.net/privacy). If you open a GitHub issue, it is public and handled under GitHub's terms.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to forwardemail.net, GitHub, RFC Editor and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Forward Email's privacy policy is at [forwardemail.net/privacy](https://forwardemail.net/privacy). When you follow a link, the site sends only the origin (timetoinbox.com) as the referrer, not the full page address.
